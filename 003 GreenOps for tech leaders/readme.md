# 003 GreenOps for Tech Leaders

> **GreenOps for Tech Leaders**
>
> Join Burendo’s Cloud Architect and Principal Consultant, Kevin Kuszyk, and UKHSA’s Head of Development Graham Whitehouse in an exclusive look at how GreenOps was embedded into the processes of creating the award-winning Public Health Data Dashboard. What is GreenOps? The term refers to the balance between operational and financial efficiency, and the lowest possible environmental impact
>
> **What will you get from the session?**
>
> - You will learn how we embedded GreenOps and Sustainability into building and rolling out a nationwide data dashboard
> - How the dashboard was architected and re-architected for Sustainability
> - How to deliver a successful project that’s both better for the environment and cost-effective
> - How the UKHSA is planning to embed GreenOps further into its delivery process
> - The importance of GreenOps and Sustainability in tech
